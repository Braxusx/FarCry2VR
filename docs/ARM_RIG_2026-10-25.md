# Wrist deformation and elbow alignment — offline checkpoint

Branch: `codex/tracking-arms-physical-steering`. Parent: `c657d9f`.
Startup marker: `contribution arm-rig-1`. No game, deployment, push or release.

## Implemented

`common/arm_rig.h` adds a pure, staged arm deformation path. The native adapter is
**experimental and default-off** (`ArmRigFix=0`). No user's INI was edited.

- Resolve case-sensitive CRC32 bone names and actual parent-array links, with
  duplicate/missing/cyclic/out-of-range rejection. Check both palms, upper arms,
  forearms, clavicles and Camera against the existing native helper selection.
  No fixed-index subtraction or new bone-poking pass.
- Each arm owns exactly 25 staged records: main UpperArm plus two upper helpers,
  Forearm plus four forearm helpers, palm plus fifteen digits, and the separate
  Pelvis-parented Elbow corrective. Clavicles, torso, camera, knees and unknown
  helpers are not written by this path. Proximity-based helper assignment is
  disabled in named mode. Unknown/mismatched rigs do not activate it.
- Solve UpperArm-origin to Forearm-origin to Hand-origin geometry. Preserve both
  lengths on the feasible elbow circle, with previous/rest/deterministic pole
  fallbacks and hemisphere continuity. Folded/coincident and unreachable targets
  have explicit endpoint policies. Right/support hands retain their target;
  an out-of-reach free left hand is clamped with its fingers as one set.
- Store elbow-pole history in the neutral body frame, not world space. Shoulder
  orientation uses the published game/body yaw basis in named mode; head position
  still supplies lean/crouch translation. This prevents head-only looking from
  directly selecting shoulder yaw. Actual native body/camera coupling still needs
  runtime validation, as do the existing shoulder offset settings.
- Swing the upper arm without importing wrist pronation into it. Extract the
  closest axial wrist residual from the full swung/target palm bases using two
  transverse vectors. Do not distribute pure wrist flex as axial twist. Unwrap
  once per source sample and distribute bounded roll through the four named
  forearm helpers by their longitudinal positions, not array order. Preserve the
  palm/weapon orientation. Default helper-roll limit is 90 degrees each way.
- Carry the elbow corrective's authored offset/basis in an elbow-anchored hinge
  frame. Stabilize its hemisphere with an authored upper-arm reference transported
  by the swing. Do not carry wrist roll into the cap. This is an explicit VR
  deformation policy, **not a recovered Dunia procedural blend**. Nearly straight
  authored poses cannot invert the cap simply by crossing the bend-plane zero.
- Stage and validate all records and history before accepting the pure solve.
  The adapter requires current physical head/hand authority. A rejected arm stage
  aborts that palette application and clears the cached aim/support override;
  final named writes are restricted to the staged records. Native write failure
  and native job races are not claimed atomic or solved.

The existing path also gains a tested antiparallel segment rotation, safe planar
frame fallback for vertical gaze and arm-mask invalidation on palette-pointer
changes. Legacy head-yaw/right-side policy otherwise remains. The named path
deliberately replaces the old `ArmsIndependent`, `UpperArmTwist` and
`LeftForearmTwist` deformation logic; existing hand angle/hold targets remain, and
`ArmWristTwistLimit` controls its helper-only axial cap. It does not implement DQS.

## Offline controls and results

Hypotheses: undefined segment/pole directions can misalign the elbow; coincident
helper assignment does not establish corrective ownership; abrupt palm-only roll
can pinch a weighted wrist. Controls include the old 180-degree endpoint failure,
pole-zero crossings, known CRC, shuffled/renumbered maps, missing/duplicate/wrong
parents, wrist flex versus pronation, fixed-palm roll and a synthetic weighted ring.
Pass gates: finite rigid stages, retained segment lengths/endpoints, continuous
pole/twist history, no cap response to wrist-only motion, and no changed output or
history after late rejection. Synthetic mesh quality is not headset acceptance.

All **39 MSVC x86 Release CTest groups pass** (`/W4 /WX`): 35 numeric groups with
**81,977 checks**, plus four source-wiring guards. The nine new groups (18,991
checks) also pass GCC 16.2.0 i686 (`-O2 -Wall -Wextra -Werror`):

The ring is synthetic linear-blend skinning with two known influences and a
90-degree wrist turn. Distributed helper rotation reduces its radius loss versus
the no-distribution control; it does not measure FC2 skin weights, sleeves or the
actual renderer. No claim of full volume preservation or solved mesh pinching.

Rebuilt ignored mutant headers deliberately remove wrist distribution, pole/cap
hemisphere protection and body-local history, and carry wrist rotation into the
cap. Singular, wrist, cap, mesh and history tests fail with exit 1 (respectively
1, 4,000, 2, 360 and 2 failed checks). Mutants are never part of the DLL.

## First runtime gate — not performed

After an authorized deployment/run, require the exact module marker/path and
`arm-rig-1 enabled=1`, `map=1` with expected landmarks/caps, then `staged=1` counters.
Mapping or staging alone is not proof that later native writers retain the output.
The opt-in settings are `[VR] ArmRigFix=1`, `ArmIK=1`,
`ArmWristTwistLimit=90`; `ArmRigFix=0` reverts the new deformation policy. No setting
was enabled in a live config here. Compare the same poses against the parent build.

Acceptance needs both wrists (flex/roll and wrap crossing), sleeve/cap shape,
elbow alignment near extension/folding, large body turns, head-only looks,
controller/weapon/support endpoints, grip/calibration changes, phone/map/reload,
tracking/focus/recenter loss and recovery. Verify native worker/palette ownership
and later writers. Close only the owned game process after testing.

R1 scheduling, R4/R10 native lifetime/cache provenance, R9 image refusal, precise
skin-weight quality and vehicle/seated shoulder policy remain open. This checkpoint
is not a release candidate or a claim that the wonky rig is headset-fixed. Roll
back this checkpoint to `c657d9f`; no deployed file or user config needs restoring.
